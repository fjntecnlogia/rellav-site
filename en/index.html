THE PROBLEM

Do any of these sound familiar?

These are the most common problems for companies with a field team. Most still live with them every single day.

📋

"My rep still writes orders on paper"

Typos, rework, and information that arrives too late. A process that costs money every day.

→ Human error · Rework · Wasted time

📍

"I don't know where my reps are"

No route visibility, no visit log, no control over who was served and who was left behind.

→ Inefficient routes · No visibility

💸

"I only find out after the sale"

Out of stock, wrong price, canceled order. The customer footing the bill can't be the last to know.

→ Unhappy customer · Lost sale

FEATURES

Everything your team needs.
In a single app.

From the field to your ERP — no rework, no errors, no wasted time.

👥 Customers

An organized book of business. A team focused on the right accounts.

Segment your accounts by Curva A, B e C so your team prioritizes the accounts that actually matter.

  • Search by name, tax ID, or phone
  • A/B/C tiering with color coding
  • Call, email, and map right from the customer record
  • Full history of visits and orders
  • Credit data and limits per customer
📦 Catalog

An up-to-date catalog, always in your rep's hand.

Products with photo, price, and real-time inventory. Your rep walks into the meeting prepared — no surprises.

  • Photos, prices, and availability in real time
  • Search by name, SKU, or barcode
  • Built-in barcode scanner
  • Multiple price lists per customer
  • List or grid view
🛒 Orders & Quotes

An order in 60 seconds. A quote approved over WhatsApp.

Build the order and send the quote over WhatsApp. Your customer opens the link, reviews the items, and approves with one tap — with nothing to install. The confirmed order lands straight in your ERP.

  • Order in a few taps, with pricing pulled automatically
  • Send the quote over WhatsApp in one tap
  • Customer approves or declines from the link — no login
  • Straight into the ERP — zero re-entry
  • Order status in real time
🏷️ Pricing & Coupons

The right price per customer. Coupons that lift the ticket.

Each customer sees the price on their own list — with wholesale tiers, validity dates, and discounts. And you can create rule-based coupons (minimum value, expiry, account set) to drive campaigns.

  • Price lists by customer and segment, with validity dates
  • Wholesale pricing and min/max discount per product
  • Discount coupons (% or fixed amount) with rules
  • Applied automatically to the order, with the total recalculated
  • Usage limits per coupon and per customer
🗺️ Live map

See where your team is and who hasn't been visited yet.

Reps and customers on the map in real time. Plan smart routes and make sure no key account goes unattended.

  • Field reps plotted in real time
  • Customers mapped by location
  • Visit route planning
  • Filters by tier, status, and last visit

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does setup take?

Basic setup takes about 15 minutes: sign-up, importing customers and products, and downloading the app. The ERP integration, on the other hand, depends on the project — which system you run, what needs to sync, and the access granted on your side. We set the timeline with you during the walkthrough, and technical support stays with you throughout.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Rellav is offline-first: your rep looks up customers, catalog, and prices and builds orders even with no signal. Everything is saved on the device and syncs automatically as soon as the connection returns — no order gets lost along the way.

How does the ERP integration work?

Via API or a standardized import file. Our technical team configures it with you — at no extra cost, on any plan.

Can I see the platform before signing up?

Yes. We run a guided demo with your team, using your operation's real scenario. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll set a time.

What if I need more reps?

Just reach out. We adjust the plan with no hassle, no long contracts, and no penalties.

Is support available in English?

Yes. WhatsApp support, in English, included in every plan. You talk to a real person — no chatbot.
